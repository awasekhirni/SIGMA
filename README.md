Signature Intelligence and Geospatial Measurement Architecture(SIGMA)
- The Sum of All Signatures

  
Syed, Awase Khirni Ph.D.

An open source unified, multidomain intelligence fusion platform that synthesizes measurement 
and signature intelligence with geospatial intelligence to detect, charaterise and track low-unobservable
or clandestine activities. It is aimed to resolve the "needle in a haystack" challenge of identifying 
stealth platforms, underground facilities, and anomalous environmental changes by correlating subtle 
physical signatures with precise geospatial context. It is aimed to provide transparent civil security and environmental monitoring 
for all countries transparently to safeguard civilians worldwide.
